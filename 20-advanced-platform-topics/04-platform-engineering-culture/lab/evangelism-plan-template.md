## Worked example (reference — resistance response)

> **Objection:** A senior engineer says publicly, *"The platform slows me down — I can
> provision what I need on the cloud in five minutes. Why adopt it?"*
>
> **Response.** "You're right that raw provisioning is fast, and for a genuinely
> special case going direct can be the correct call — the platform isn't a cage. But
> provisioning speed isn't where the platform earns its keep; it's everything *after*:
> secure defaults, observability and alerts wired in, compliance by default, on-call
> support, and not re-solving problems other teams already solved. Concretely, teams
> on the golden path went from ~9 days to ~1 day to first production deploy and had
> ~40% fewer change-failures last quarter. If you go direct, the guardrails (signed
> images, policy) still apply for safety, and you own the rest yourself — that's a
> legitimate choice. And tell me what's missing that makes direct faster for you;
> that's exactly what I want to pave next." (No mandate anywhere — the critic becomes a
> roadmap source, in the open.)
