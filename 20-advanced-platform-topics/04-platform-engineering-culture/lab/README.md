# Lab — Draft a Platform Evangelism and Communication Plan

> Produce the plan a platform lead uses to win adoption: audience and channels, a
> sustainable communication cadence, three success stories to tell, and a reasoned
> response to the "why not just use the cloud directly?" objection.

**Estimated time:** ~45–60 minutes
**Type:** Design
**Deliverable:** A completed [evangelism and communication plan](./evangelism-plan-template.md), including a worked resistance-scenario response.

## Objectives

- Define the audience and the evangelism + marketing channels to reach them.
- Design a **communication cadence** the team can actually sustain for a year.
- Draft three concrete, credited **success stories** (with before/after numbers).
- Write an evidence-based response to the core **resistance** objection — without
  resorting to a mandate.

## Prerequisites

- [Module 20.4 theory](../README.md) — culture, the empire anti-pattern, evangelism,
  marketing, handling resistance, open roadmaps, careers.
- Helpful: [Module 01.2 Platform as a Product](../../../01-platform-foundations/02-platform-as-a-product/)
  (adoption as north star) and [01.5 Platform Team Topology](../../../01-platform-foundations/05-platform-team-topology/)
  (enabling vs gatekeeping). Impact numbers borrow from
  [Module 20.3](../../03-platform-engineering-metrics/).
- Nothing to install. A real platform you know makes this richer; a fictional one is
  fine (reuse "Northwind" from the Module 20.1 lab if you like).

## Steps

1. **Copy the template.** Open
   [`evangelism-plan-template.md`](./evangelism-plan-template.md) and copy it to a
   working file.

2. **Name the audience and where they are.** Who are you trying to reach (new hires?
   skeptical senior engineers? a specific team?), and which channels do they actually
   read/attend? Design for *them*, not for your convenience.

3. **Design a sustainable cadence.** Fill the weekly / monthly / quarterly rhythm.
   The test: *could the team keep this up for a year without heroics?* If not, make it
   smaller. A reliable 3-line weekly changelog beats a quarterly epic nobody writes.

4. **Draft three success stories.** Each needs a named team, a real *before → after*
   number (time-to-prod, deploy frequency, incidents — Module 20.3), and credit to the
   team. Stories travel; feature lists don't.

5. **Plan the community of practice.** How will you seed and support cross-team
   **champions**? What do they get (early access, credit, influence) in return for
   advocating inside their teams?

6. **Write the resistance response.** Answer *"why use the platform when I can use the
   cloud directly?"* using the five moves: acknowledge the legitimate core, make the
   evidence-based paved-road case, offer an escape hatch (guardrails intact), turn the
   need into roadmap, and — explicitly — **no mandate**.

7. **Empire self-check.** Review your plan for any place you're reaching for control or
   mandate instead of enablement. Replace it with a guardrail, a self-service path, or
   an evidence-based pitch.

## Expected result

A one-to-two-page plan that a platform lead could execute on Monday: a clear audience,
channels, a cadence the team can sustain, three tellable success stories, a champions
plan, and a resistance response that would actually change a skeptic's mind *without*
pulling rank. It should read as **enabling**, never empire-building.

## Cleanup

> Nothing to tear down — this is a pure design lab with no running resources and no
> cloud spend. Keep the plan; it's a portfolio artifact and a real operating document.

## Reflection

- Is your cadence genuinely sustainable, or did you over-promise a launch blitz that
  will fizzle?
- Do your success stories lead with **outcomes** (their numbers) or with features
  (your work)?
- In the resistance response, did you ever reach for authority? If so, what evidence
  or escape hatch could replace it?
- Where in your plan did the **empire reflex** show up — and how did you convert
  control into enablement?

## Stretch goals (optional)

- **Write the two-minute demo script.** Outline the golden-path demo that turns "two
  days" into "two minutes," beat by beat.
- **Draft the quarterly roadmap post.** Write the actual "shipped / in progress /
  next / invite your input" update you'd publish, in the open.
- **Sketch the career ladder.** For your team, write one line per level (engineer →
  senior → staff → principal) describing the *scope of impact* that defines it, and
  confirm none of the levels reward empire-building.
